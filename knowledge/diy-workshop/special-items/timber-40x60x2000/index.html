Timber 40 × 60 × 2000 mm

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Check soon

The goal is to find timber measuring 2000 mm long with a 40 × 60 mm cross-section, preferably to collect from a German supplier as close to Enschede as possible.

This search is deliberately German-first. The 40 × 60 mm section is a normal listed German timber size, while comparable Dutch construction timber is often sold using different nominal and finished dimensions.

Best nearby lead: Wigbels in Gronau-Epe

Bau + Holzmarkt Wigbels is currently the most promising nearby supplier found.

Their own stock list explicitly includes:

  • Fichte/Tanne 40 × 60 mm as Rahmen;
  • Fichte/Tanne 40 × 60 mm battens;
  • timber directly from stock;
  • cutting and other timber-processing services.

Address: Hauskamp 11, 48599 Gronau (Westfalen).

The published standard lengths for the 40 × 60 mm battens are 3.0 to 5.0 m, so 2000 mm is not shown as a standard stocked length. Because Wigbels offers cutting, the practical question is whether they can supply or cut a 40 × 60 mm piece to exactly 2000 mm.

This is much closer to the actual requirement than searching Dutch timber merchants for a nominal equivalent.
