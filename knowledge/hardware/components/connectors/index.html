Connector terminology

Two different properties describe a connector: its contacts and how it is mounted. Do not use male/female as a synonym for plug/socket or cable/chassis.

TermMeaningDutch term
MaleHas protruding pin contacts.Mannelijk, pinnen
FemaleHas receiving socket contacts.Vrouwelijk, bussen
Cable plug / cable connectorLoose connector attached to a cable; can have male or female contacts.Kabelstekker / kabeldeel; female cable versions are often called contrastekker.
Receptacle / panel-mount connectorConnector mounted to a panel or enclosure; can have male or female contacts.Chassisdeel / inbouwdeel
Front mountMounted at a panel opening, typically with a flange.Frontmontage / inbouw
Surface / angled mountConnector on a mounting base, with the mating direction at an angle.Opbouwmontage / haaks chassisdeel

Naming rule: describe both contact gender and mounting. For example, “male panel-mount receptacle” is a mannelijk chassisdeel (not automatically a female socket). In supplier catalogues, socket sometimes describes the fixed mounting style, while male still identifies its pins.

For examples, see Hirschmann ST connectors.
