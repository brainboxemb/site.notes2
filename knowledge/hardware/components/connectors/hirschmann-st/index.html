Hirschmann ST connectors

Created: 31 Oct 2026 Updated: 31 Oct 2026 Checked: 31 Oct 2026Current

The ST series is sold under the Hirschmann brand, which is part of Belden. The four ST 200 models below are 2+PE connectors (two contacts plus protective earth). They have a keyed mating profile; the 200 is a variant designation, not a pin count.

Mating combinations

Of these four variants, STAS 200 (male, cable) mates with STAK 200 (female, cable). The STAK 200 also mates with the STASEI 200 (male, front mount) and STASAP 200 (male, angled panel mount). A male panel connector needs a female mating half; it is not female merely because it is called a receptacle.

For other counterparts, Hirschmann also lists female panel versions STAKEI 200 and STAKAP 200 in its ST series.

Electrical safety: Check the exact coding, pin assignment, ratings, locking bracket and sealing against the manufacturer’s documentation. The protection rating depends on the assembled, correctly mated combination. Do not connect or disconnect under load.
