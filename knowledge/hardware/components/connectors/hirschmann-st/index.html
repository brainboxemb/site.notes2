Hirschmann ST connectors

Created: 31 Oct 2026 Checked: 31 Oct 2026Current

The Hirschmann ST 200 family includes cable connectors and panel-mounted receptacles. These four variants use a 2+PE contact arrangement (two conductors plus protective earth). The 200 variants have a coding feature.

STAS 200MaleCableStraight
STAK 200FemaleCableStraight
STASEI 200MalePanel, front-mountedStraight

Angled, male receptacle with a two-hole mounting base. Hirschmann part number: 932046106.

Product reference: STASAP 200 at TME.

Note: Check the mating connector, keying, wiring, contact ratings and sealing in the manufacturer’s datasheet before use. An IP rating may depend on correct mounting and a properly mated connection.

Manufacturer reference: Hirschmann ST series (Belden).
